EMAIL FORM
The contact form uses FormSubmit to deliver enquiries to info@alphaccpk.com.
1. Open the website through its hosted URL (not a file:// address).
2. Submit one test enquiry using an email address you control.
3. Open info@alphaccpk.com and check Inbox and Spam for FormSubmit's activation email.
4. Click its confirmation link.
5. Submit another test enquiry and confirm it arrives before promoting the form.
The initial activation message is not proof of enquiry delivery. Delivery has not been tested from this workspace.
Submissions are processed by FormSubmit. No email password or API key is stored in the code.
Required fields: Name, Email and Message. FormSubmit's default CAPTCHA is enabled.
